# Summary
What if you could (should) only purchase the minimal kit to get out on the water? What would that be and why? Is this even possible given the mix of conditions and needed skills for kayakers? Perhaps it's purchasing a 'quiver killer' or 'swiss-army knife' type of boat, e.g., a boat that does it all well, but likely doesn't excel at any particular conditinon what would you pick. For this list we've hedged a bit by offering some options, e.g., a compositie boat and a plastic boat, although the argument could be made that plastic boats tend to be better all rounders, and require less care.
