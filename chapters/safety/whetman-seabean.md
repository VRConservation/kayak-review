# Summary
> 'I hate 'em.' Stuart Leslie on long tows

Ok, well Stu doesn't like any long tows, any you can see his point, they're messy, the lines get in the way, they're hard to stow, and they're not necessary for most rescues if you're fast, efficient, and know how to use a contact tow, well.

However, you need a towline and this one fits the bill from thoughtful easy to use carabiners to a good lenght. It's easy to stow the line once you've used it due to the shape of the bean. The belt could use an extra loop to keep the tail end fixed, I added an elastic hair band to do this and sometimes the oval shape of the carabiner is awkward to remove from the belt and deploy, but other than that, I really like this towline. The price does seem high at first glance, but all towlines are a bit pricey. Could you make your own, heck yes, but this is a solid line that will work over a long time from a UK based company.

# Buyer
Anyone that needs a compact, deployable towline.
