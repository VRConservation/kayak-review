# Summary
I have to admit I have a love/hate relationship with the Whetman Sea Contact Tow MK11. While paddling, it has the tendency to unravel or come undone from its origami-like stow setup, and the miserly amount of velcro used to secure it doesn't help. However, the carabiners and quick deployment make it easy to use when it's needed. It's also highly adjustable, so usable on multiple boats. This contact tow would be perfect if the folding/stowage were improved substantially, mostly by making the velcro closure larger/longer and more secure when holding the extra towline.

Before purchasing this unit, I made contact with climbing equipment and rope, but went through a number of carabiners that froze after saltwater contact or that weren't the right shape for easy deployment. None of them had a quick-release toggle, but that would have been easy to add. Finding the right pieces will make a more customized and perhaps more stowable contact tow, but how much time and money do you spend on that before getting it right?

# Buyer
Any paddler, especially trip leaders and coaches, should have this safety device for quick retrieval of wayward boats or easier contact tows with casualties.
