# Summary
Epic are one of the great makers of surfskis, that boat you see folks in South Africa riding downwinders in massive waves. They are fun to paddle and the cost of entry is low with an open cockpit and they're lightweight (you can literally carry them with one hand). I've tried the V8, 9 and 10, each higher number slightly faster, longer, and skinnier and found that the V9 hits the sweet spot for speed and ability to stay upright without having to focus too much. The V10 is paddle-able but I've found I had to focus all my attention on not tipping it. Of course you have to paddle these with a wing paddle or you'll be uncool. There are newer touring version that also have hatches.

One great aspect of the surfskis is simple open cockpit. It makes re-entry after capsize very easy and subtracts the need for a spraydeck. Of course this means you get pretty wet and paddle in a puddle while out, but this is a minor inconvenience for speed and accessing downwind waves. On flat water, without much effort, I've found that I average 6-7 knots over a 10 km paddle in a V9. So they are speedy!

# Buyer
A paddler who likes speed and paddling to waves or someone that doesn't feel comfortable locked into a kayak in a spraydeck.

# Specs
Various sizes and models to choose from and other makers such as Fenn, Nordic, and others.
