# Summary
The Aries will launch and ride any wave you want and just wants to run waves all day. It's short length, planing hull, and bow/stern rocker make playing in races and surf so much fun. the boat also turns on a dime to catch and point yourself in whatever direction you need. The cockpit outfitting is super comfortable and the ability to dial thigh and hip braces for a comfortable but controllable setup is really great. This isn't an expedition boat, but the hatches hold a lot of kit and could let you take overnight stuff to set up a basecamp near a race, e.g., St. Katherines Point, then go run the race w/o having to paddle a gazillion miles in a day to access it.

If you come from a river background, paddling this boat won't be a big deal. However if you're used to paddling longer sea kayaks that track well, the boat takes a little getting used to and often requires running it with the skeg down. My version is a bit older and this is fixed in newer P&G boats, but the skeg slider is exactly where my left leg comes down when landing, causing the skeg to drop exactly when you don't want it to and leading to the inevitable comment from your fellow paddlers of 'Vance you're skeg's down, mate.' I know, it's terrible.

# Buyer
Any paddler that wants to have a great playboat that surfs incredibly well and handles any kind of rough water. In fact, could you river run it? Maybe not with the fiberglass!
