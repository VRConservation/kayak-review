# Summary
From the moment I tried out this paddle from a friend to the present, this has been my go-to paddle. I've used this paddle all over for short and long distances, from paddles of a few kilometers to over 70 km. It's a solid paddle that fits right in my hands and gives confidence for forward strokes and ruddering. The Omega is lightweight and durable, even withstanding hits on rocks (although it has chipped in a few spots for paddling in places I shouldn't have with this paddle). The leverlock is easy to use and simple. I've only had to tighten it once or twice, and the paddles are removable from the shaft, making travel or replacement easy. One additional plus is the degree of customization that's possible to dial in the correct paddle size from blade to length.

I would put the price at medium for paddles. Paddles may seem pricey to the beginner, but when you think about how much you use one, the paddle is your engine, steering wheel, and brakes. It is getting the paddle right and spending some simoleons on a good one. Word of warning tho, don't spend a lot until you try out a lot of different paddles. Just ask folks to borrow theirs for a few minutes on each trip and get a feel for what you like.
