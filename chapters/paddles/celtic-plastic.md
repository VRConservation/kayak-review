# Summary
This paddle is a facsimile of the Celtic Omega but with nylon blades that you can bash along rocks, shorelines, or beach landings without a care in the world. The carbon shaft has the same stiffness. You do notice a little bit of weight penalty, but the ease of mind when taking into rocks or where hard stuff might not work very well with carbon paddles. I have nothing but praise for this paddle; it's great for any beginner to advanced paddle, feels good in hand, and strokes confidently in the water. Make sure to choose your size correctly; 650 works well for most, but you might find 600 is better. I've started to feel like the 208 minimum on this blade is a bit long; however, the NDK factory can shorten paddles to the length you want.
