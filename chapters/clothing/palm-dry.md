# Summary
This is more of a first-impressions review, since I've only worn the drysuit on the water a couple of dozen times since purchasing it. Overall, it's very comfortable, easy to don, and dry. It has an extra chest pocket for incidentals.

At first, the shoulder zipper is difficult to get used to, as the shoulders on my version are fairly generous and move around as you try to zip. However, unlike many other suits, the zip comes around toward the front of the shoulder, making it easier to access at the beginning and end to complete the zip yourself (it's still more awkward than a waist or cross-chest zip!). All materials are high-quality, and the design is great, with few seams. I like the waist 'belt' rather than suspenders to keep the lower end of the suit up, though it does add an extra bit of fiddle to pull it up.

The sizing on these suits is generous. I fit it according to the sizing chart, and it is a little large. Probably a good idea to try on in a shop to make sure it's right. The socks are massive, probably size 14 or 15 in large. I got around this by wearing oversocks to rein them in. The pee zip is slightly narrow, although that makes it easier to re-zip.

I will add to this review over time.

# Buyer
Anyone who wants a mid-range priced drysuit that is comfortable and does the job. Hopefully, it is as durable as they say.
