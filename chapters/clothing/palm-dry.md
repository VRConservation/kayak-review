# Summary
This is more of a first impressions review since I've only worn the drysuit on the water a couple of dozen times since purchasing it. Overall it's very comfortable, easy to don and is dry. It has an extra chest pocket for incidentals.

At first putting the shoulder zipper is difficult to get used to as the shoulders on my version are fairly generous and move around as you're trying to zip. However, unlike many other suits, the zip comes around toward the front of the shoulder and allows easier initial and final access to complete the zip yourself (it's still more awkward than a waist or cross chest zip!). All materials are high quality and design is great with few seams. I like the waist 'belt' rather than suspenders to keep the lower end of the suit up, although it does add an extra amount of fiddle to pull that up.

The sizing is generous on these suits. I fit it according to the sizing chart and it is a little large. Probably a good idea to try on in a shop to make sure it's right. The socks are massive, probably size 14 or 15 in the large. I got around this by wearing oversocks to rein them in. The pee zip is slightly narrow, although that makes it easier to re-zip.

I will add to this review over time.

# Buyer
Anyone that wants a mid-range priced drysuit that is comfortable and does the job. Hopefully it is as durable as they say.
